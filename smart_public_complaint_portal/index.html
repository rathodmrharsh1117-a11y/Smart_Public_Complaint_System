GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to report an issue?

No. You can submit a report without signing up. Name and contact details are optional.

How do I check the status of my complaint?

Open “Track complaints” and enter the reference ID shown after you submit your report.

Is my report sent to a real authority?

No. This is a demonstration version: reports are stored only in your browser. A live version would connect to a municipal backend.

What should I write in the description?

Give the exact location, when you noticed the problem and how it affects people. Avoid sensitive personal data.

What if it is an emergency?

Call 112 (or 100, 101, 108) immediately. Do not use this portal for urgent danger.
